Why you need this

What would you do with the stems?

Learn your instrument
Solo the guitar, piano or bass on any song you are studying and play along. Mute everything else and the part you are learning stops being a guessing game. Rewind a hundred times, the app never gets tired.
Sing
Any track becomes your backing band. The karaoke preset strips the vocals instantly, at home, at a party, or for the cover you have been planning to post. No per-track fees, no waiting on a service.
Remix and sample
Pull acapellas and drum loops for flips and mashups. Export as WAV, drop them into your DAW, and get to work. It runs on your machine, so your ideas never touch someone else's server.
Study the mix
Hear what the drummer actually played. Isolate the bass line you could never pick out of the mix. Solo the piano part on your favorite record and learn the voicings by ear.

The fine print

Honest details.

Everything stays local
Audio is downloaded and separated on your machine. No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry.
First run is the big one
Setup installs a private Python engine, about 2GB, one time. After that, songs split in minutes and work offline.
Requirements
macOS 12+ (Apple Silicon) or Windows 10/11, with Python 3.9+ available. ffmpeg is bundled.
Open source, MIT
Source code is on GitHub. Powered by demucs, yt-dlp and the Web Audio API.
