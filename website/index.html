How it works

Three steps. No accounts, no uploads.

01

Paste a link, or search

YouTube search is built in. Pick a track without ever opening a browser tab.

02

Pick your instruments

Vocals, drums, bass, other, plus guitar and piano. Only what you ask for gets produced.

03

Play, mute, solo, export

Stems play synced with the video. Karaoke and acapella are presets, not projects.

The karaoke moment

Every song, minus the singer.

One preset click removes the vocals from any track. Or flip it: solo the voice and nothing else. Instrumentals and acapellas, straight from the original mix.

The fine print

Honest details.

Everything stays local
Audio is downloaded and separated on your machine. No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry.
First run is the big one
Setup installs a private Python engine, about 2GB, one time. After that, songs split in minutes and work offline.
Requirements
macOS 12+ (Apple Silicon) or Windows 10/11, with Python 3.9+ available. ffmpeg is bundled.
Open source, MIT
Source code is on GitHub. Powered by demucs, yt-dlp and the Web Audio API.
